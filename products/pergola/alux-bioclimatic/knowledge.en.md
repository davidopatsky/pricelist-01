# ALUX Bioclimatic

## Description

ALUX Bioclimatic – bioclimatic pergola with adjustable louvres. Frame RAL 7016, louvres RAL 9006 / 7016 Axalta fine texture.

## Colours and finishes

Frame RAL 7016. Louvres RAL 9006 / RAL 7016 Axalta fine texture. Other frame colours on request, other louvre colours individually.

## Specification

- Dimension axes: width × depth (mm)
- Number of louvres: 11–39 (29 variants)
