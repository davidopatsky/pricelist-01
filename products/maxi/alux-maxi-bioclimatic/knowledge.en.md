# ALUX MAXI Bioclimatic

## Description

ALUX MAXI Bioclimatic – freestanding bioclimatic pergola with adjustable louvres, massive perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm, UNO (1 field) or DUO (2 fields) version depending on size, span up to 7.5 m without a middle post, frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Colors and finishes

Frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure. Louvres RAL 9006 or RAL 7016 Axalta structure. Other frame colors on request, other louvre colors individually.

## Specification

- Freestanding structure
- Version by size: up to 7 × 4 m UNO on 4 posts; up to 7.5 × 5.5 m DUO on 4 posts; up to 8 × 5.5 m DUO on 6 posts (3 on each side)
- UNO – roof without a middle beam, rafters parallel to the depth, slope to the front
- DUO – roof with a middle beam, saddle roof sloping to the sides, rafters parallel to the width
- Main perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 5 mm)
- Posts 150 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 4 mm)
- Span up to 7.5 m without a middle post
- Max. size 8.0 × 5.5 m
- Roof: adjustable louvres; UNO 1 field with 1 motor, DUO 2 fields – the louvres can be operated together or each field separately
- Motor: price includes the standard Somfy IO motor, can be replaced with Somfy WT
- Glazing: for spans over 7 m frameless glazing is recommended (operating deflection of the structure 4–5 mm); the 150 mm bottom profile allows wider tracks
