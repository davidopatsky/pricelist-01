# ALUX MAXI Bioclimatic UNO

## Description

ALUX MAXI Bioclimatic UNO – bioclimatic pergola with adjustable louvres in the UNO version (1 field), massive perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm, 2 posts, span up to 7.59 m without a middle post, frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure. The profiles have space for integrated perimeter LED lighting (optional surcharge).

## Colors and finishes

Frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure. Louvres RAL 9006 or RAL 7016 Axalta structure. Other frame colors on request, other louvre colors individually.

## Specification

- Zones by size: up to 7.59 × 4.5 m UNO - 2 posts; up to 7.99 × 4.5 m UNO - 3 posts
- UNO – roof without a middle beam, rafters parallel to the depth, slope to the front
- Main perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 5 mm)
- Posts 150 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 4 mm)
- Span up to 7.59 m without a middle post
- Max. size 7.99 × 4.5 m
- Louvre size series in 200 mm steps across the width (1,990 – 7,990 mm)
- Roof: adjustable louvres, 1 field with 1 motor
- Motor: price includes the standard Somfy IO motor, can be replaced with Somfy WT
- Glazing: for spans over 7 m frameless glazing is recommended (operating deflection of the structure 4–5 mm); the 150 mm bottom profile allows wider tracks
