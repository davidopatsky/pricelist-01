# ALUX MAXI BIO

## Description

ALUX MAXI BIO – bioclimatic pergola with adjustable louvres in a single undivided field, massive perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm, span up to 8 m without a middle post, frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Colors and finishes

Frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Specification

- Main perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 5 mm)
- Posts 150 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 4 mm), always 4 posts
- Max. size 8.0 × 4.5 m
- Roof: 1 independent segment with 1 motor, no middle dividing profile
- Recommendation: always anchor the pergola to the house for better stability, especially where gusty wind is a risk
- Glazing: for spans over 7 m frameless glazing is recommended (operating deflection of the structure 4–5 mm); the 150 mm bottom profile allows wider tracks
