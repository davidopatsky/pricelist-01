# ALUX MAXI FUSION

## Description

ALUX MAXI FUSION – freestanding pergola with a divided roof: a middle dividing profile splits the roof into 2 fields with different roofing – BIO / Glass, BIO / polycarbonate or Glass / Thermo; massive perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm, span up to 8 m without a middle post, frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Colors and finishes

Frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Specification

- Freestanding structure on 4 posts
- Main perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 5 mm)
- Posts 150 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 4 mm)
- Span up to 8 m without a middle post
- Max. size 8.0 × 6.0 m
- Roof: 2 fields separated by a middle dividing profile, roofing combinations BIO / Glass, BIO / polycarbonate, Glass / Thermo
- Glazing: for spans over 7 m frameless glazing is recommended (operating deflection of the structure 4–5 mm); the 150 mm bottom profile allows wider tracks
