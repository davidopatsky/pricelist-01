# ALUX MAXI Fusion

## Description

ALUX MAXI Fusion – freestanding pergola, always in the DUO version: the middle beam in the center divides the roof into 2 fields with different roofing – BIO / Glass, BIO / Carbo, Thermo / Glass or Thermo / Carbo; massive perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm, span up to 7.5 m without a middle post (up to a depth of 4.5 m), frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Colors and finishes

Frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Specification

- Freestanding structure
- Version by size: up to 7.5 × 4.5 m DUO on 4 posts; up to 8 × 5 m DUO on 6 posts (3 on each side)
- DUO – roof with a middle beam, saddle roof sloping to the sides, rafters parallel to the width
- Main perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 5 mm)
- Posts 150 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 4 mm)
- Span up to 7.5 m without a middle post (up to a depth of 4.5 m)
- Max. size 8.0 × 5.0 m
- Version: always DUO – the middle beam is always in the center
- Roof: 2 fields with any combination of roofing – BIO / Glass, BIO / Carbo, Thermo / Glass, Thermo / Carbo (4 price lists)
- Glazing: for spans over 7 m frameless glazing is recommended (operating deflection of the structure 4–5 mm); the 150 mm bottom profile allows wider tracks
