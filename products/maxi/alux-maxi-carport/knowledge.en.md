# ALUX MAXI CARPORT

## Description

ALUX MAXI CARPORT – freestanding carport with a span of up to 8 m without a middle post, at 8 × 6 m it comfortably fits up to 3 cars; roof sloping inwards to the middle load-bearing profile, clean cubic lines from the outside, roof of 4 cm insulated thermo panel, frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Colors and finishes

Frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Specification

- Freestanding structure on 4 posts
- Main perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 5 mm)
- Posts 150 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 4 mm)
- Span up to 8 m without a middle post
- Max. size 8.0 × 6.0 m — comfortably fits up to 3 cars
- Roof: saddle shape – massive middle load-bearing profile, sloping to the right and left
- Water drainage: integrated, requires at least 2 drainage posts
- Roofing: 4 cm insulated thermo panel; can be combined with polycarbonate or glass for more light
