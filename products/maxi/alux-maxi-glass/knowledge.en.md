# ALUX MAXI Glass

## Description

ALUX MAXI Glass – freestanding pergola with a glass roof, massive perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm, UNO or DUO version depending on size, span up to 7.5 m without a middle post, frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Colors and finishes

Frame RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

## Specification

- Freestanding structure
- Zones by size: up to 7 × 3.5 m UNO - 2 posts; up to 7.5 × 5 m DUO - 2 posts; up to 8 × 5 m DUO - 3 posts
- UNO – roof without a middle beam, rafters parallel to the depth, slope to the front
- DUO – roof with a middle beam, saddle roof sloping to the sides, rafters parallel to the width
- Main perimeter profile 300 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 5 mm)
- Posts 150 × 150 mm (wall thickness up to 4 mm)
- Span up to 7.5 m without a middle post
- Max. size 8.0 × 5.0 m
- Roofing: glass
- Glazing: for spans over 7 m frameless glazing is recommended (operating deflection of the structure 4–5 mm); the 150 mm bottom profile allows wider tracks
