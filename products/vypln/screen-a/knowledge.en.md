# Screen roller blinds A

## Description

Screen roller blinds A – square box made of extruded aluminium. The end bar caps reach into the guide rails, so they do not disturb the overall look of the screen roller blind. A reinforcing edge runs along the underside of the inspection flap. Thanks to special fabrics, you keep visual contact with the surroundings.

## Colors and finishes

Box 120 × 120 mm, RAL 7016 Axalta structure.

Standard screen colors (VENYS) – no surcharge:

- **Silver** – RAL 9006
- **Brown** – RAL 8014
- **Ultra white** – RAL 9016
- **Anthracite** – RAL 7016
- **Anthracite structure** – RAL 7016S

Coating in another RAL color: surcharge per box.

- Box 120 – square, made of extruded aluminium
- Motor: price includes the standard Somfy IO motor
- Fabrics included in the price: Soltis Horizon 86, Sergé 600, Sergé 3%
